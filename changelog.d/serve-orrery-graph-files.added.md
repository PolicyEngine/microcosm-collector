Serve Orrery graph files to microcosm-runs alone: `GET /v1/orrery-graphs?run_id=…&build_id=…` lists a run's or a build's recorded documents, each with its fields, its current flag (the newest `created_at` among the build's documents, ties ordered by `document_sha256`) and its effective visibility, and `GET /v1/orrery-graphs/<submission_id>/<document_sha256>/<file_name>` returns `graph.orrery.json` or an `upstream-<sha256>.json` file from the private Orrery bucket once its bytes match its recorded SHA-256 (500 otherwise, never served). Every request needs a Google-signed identity token of `COLLECTOR_ORRERY_READER_SERVICE_ACCOUNT` for the audience `microcosm-collector/orrery-graphs`, verified with google-auth against Google's certificates, which are kept as long as their `Cache-Control` allows (401 for a missing or invalid token, 403 for another account, 503 when the certificates can't be fetched), and every answer under `/v1/orrery-graphs` has `Cache-Control: private, no-store`. A document is private when its row is, or when its own run or a run that produced one of its upstream files, matched by the file's SHA-256, has a recorded submission with licensed data. The service serves the files when it has both `COLLECTOR_ORRERY_BUCKET` and `COLLECTOR_ORRERY_READER_SERVICE_ACCOUNT` and refuses to start with only one; the deploy now requires `TELEMETRY_ORRERY_READER_SERVICE_ACCOUNT` and sets both on every service revision, and neither job gets the reader account.
