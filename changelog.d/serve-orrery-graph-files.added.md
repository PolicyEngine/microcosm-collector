Serve Orrery graph files to microcosm-runs alone: `GET /v1/orrery-graphs?run_id=…&build_id=…` lists a run's or a build's recorded documents, each with its fields, its current flag (the newest `created_at` among the build's documents, ties ordered by `document_sha256` and then by `submission_id`, the larger first) and its effective visibility, and `GET /v1/orrery-graphs/<submission_id>/<document_sha256>/<file_name>` returns `graph.orrery.json` or an `upstream-<sha256>.json` file from the private Orrery bucket. A file is read twice at the generation it was found at, holding at most one 1 MiB part: first to check that its SHA-256 is the document's recorded `document_sha256` or the one in the upstream file's name, then, only on a match, to send it (500 `orrery_graph_digest_mismatch` or `orrery_graph_changed` otherwise, never served). Every request needs a Google-signed identity token of `COLLECTOR_ORRERY_READER_SERVICE_ACCOUNT` for the audience `microcosm-collector/orrery-graphs`, verified with google-auth against Google's certificates, which are kept as long as their `Cache-Control` allows, fetched again at most once a minute for an unknown key ID, kept for up to an hour past their expiry while a fetch fails, and fetched by one request at a time without holding requests they can check (401 for a missing or invalid token, 403 for another account, 503 when no usable certificates can be had). A bucket or database that doesn't answer gives 503 (`orrery_storage_unavailable`, `orrery_catalogue_unavailable`), and every answer under `/v1/orrery-graphs` has `Cache-Control: private, no-store`. A document is private when its row is, or when a recorded submission with licensed data has the run ID or the build ID of the document or of a submission that produced one of its upstream files, matched by the file's SHA-256. The service serves the files when it has both `COLLECTOR_ORRERY_BUCKET` and `COLLECTOR_ORRERY_READER_SERVICE_ACCOUNT` and refuses to start with only one; the deploy now requires `TELEMETRY_ORRERY_READER_SERVICE_ACCOUNT` and sets both on every service revision, and neither job gets the reader account.
