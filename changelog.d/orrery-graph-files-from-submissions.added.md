Publish Orrery graph files from `orrery-inputs` submissions where the worker job has its environment's private Orrery bucket (`COLLECTOR_ORRERY_BUCKET`, which the deploy sets on the worker job only from `TELEMETRY_ORRERY_BUCKET`): the worker projects the schema and execution overlay, removes every link the published document could not resolve (evidence references keep their label and SHA-256 but lose their link; `https://` links and links to submitted upstream files stay), validates the document with the pinned Orrery validator, writes it and its linked upstream files create-only under `graphs/<submission_id>/<document sha256>/`, and records it in the new `orrery_graph_files` table (migration `0003_orrery_graph_files`) with its run, build, versions and visibility, `private` for licensed builds. The worker's handlers now come from one registry, `telemetry_collector.handlers`.
